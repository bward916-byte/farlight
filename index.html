Survey Authority · Vessel FL-7

FARLIGHT

Your home star, Hearth, sits at the centre of a quiet sector. Nobody has returned from the dark beyond it with anything but rumours. Fly out, scan, land, collect, and bring the sector's story home. The further you go, the stranger — and more valuable — what you find.

Crew

Tap a slot to change who holds it. Quick start fills the best available.

Controls

Left stick steers and throttles. In space, push forward to accelerate; the ship drifts and brakes toward the stick. FIRE shoots at the auto-selected target. LAND appears when orbiting a planet. On the surface, stick moves and the FIRE button jumps; LAND returns you to the lander once you're standing on it.

Goals

Scanning is automatic when you approach a planet. Minerals and life forms sell at Hearth Station. Artifacts unlock log entries about who was here before you. Fuel burns with thrust; refuel at Hearth. Keyboard also works: WASD/arrows, space, E, L.

LOST

Rotate your device to landscape to fly.
