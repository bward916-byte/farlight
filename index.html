Survey Authority · Vessel FL-7

FARLIGHT

Hearth sits at the centre of a quiet sector. Nobody has returned from the dark beyond it with anything but rumours. Fly out, scan, land, drive, dig, and bring the sector's story home. The further you go, the stranger and more valuable what you find — and the more it will fight back.

Crew

Tap a slot to swap candidates. Quick start takes the default roster.

Space

Stick steers and throttles; the ship drifts. FIRE shoots the auto-selected target. Near a planet, ORBIT brings up sensors. Fuel burns on thrust — refuel at Hearth. Hostiles stay away from the home cluster early on; they show up once you've sold cargo and pushed out past the survey line. Buy weapons first.

Orbit

Sensors read atmosphere, gravity, temperature, minerals, and life. Pick a landing site. Landing burns fuel; heavy gravity burns more.

Surface

Drive the terrain rover. Scanner reveals deposits and life signs around you. Drive over minerals to mine them, catch life forms, and enter ruins, wrecks, and caves to explore them on foot. Watch day/night and weather — storms cut scanner range, nights hide hazards. Return to the lander to lift off.

Cargo

Holds fill by mass. Sell at Hearth. Artifacts weigh nothing and unlock the log.

Keyboard: WASD/arrows, Space = fire/jump, E = action, Tab = ship.
