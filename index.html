Hearth sits at the centre of a quiet sector. Nobody has come back from the dark with anything but rumours. Fly out, scan, land, dig, trade — the further you go, the stranger and more valuable what you find, and the harder it fights back.

Left stick steers and throttles; the ship drifts and brakes toward the stick. FIRE shoots the auto-selected target. Fuel burns on thrust — refuel at Hearth, skim a star's corona to scoop fuel (heat and hull risk), or run dry and pay for a tow. Destroyed ships shed scrap you can fly through to collect. If you can't pay a fee, the Authority issues a loan and withholds half of every sale. MAP opens the star map: drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap a star to set an autopilot course. Only explored space is charted; some systems stay hidden until you are close.

Hostiles stay out of the home cluster until you have sold cargo and pushed past the survey line. When pirates close in, your Comms officer can bluff, pay, or jam and run — or you fight. Red dust clouds halve sensors and throw debris; the universe edge is an impassable star cloud. Mining is the money early; life forms pay only when rare.

Near a planet, ORBIT shows sensors: biosphere, atmosphere, gravity, temperature, minerals, life, anomalies. Landing burns fuel scaled by gravity. On the surface you drive the rover; the scanner reveals deposits and life signs around you. MINE, CATCH, or ENTER ruins, wrecks, caves and colonies to explore on foot — jump with FIRE, leave at either edge. Watch day/night, storms, lightning and whiteouts. Return to the lander to lift off. The rover burns charge — turn back at 50% or you lose it and pay for a replacement.

Holds fill by tonnage. Sell at Hearth, or to Veil traders for more. Buy weapons, shields, cargo pods, scanner range, rover gear. Three alien races hail you — greet, trade, take contracts, listen, or threaten; standing changes how they treat you. Five crew skills grow through play and flash on screen when they level. Distant wrecks hide alien weapons. Find the Orrery and earn the Umbra's trust to reach Consumed Hearth.

Keyboard: WASD/arrows, Space fire/jump, E action, Tab ship, M map.
